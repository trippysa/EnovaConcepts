Advanced Electronic Materials for Earth and Far Beyond

Beyond Conventional Approaches

Enova Concepts is veteran-owned small business based in San Antonio, TX which develops next-generation electronic materials design and fabrication techniques.

Our mission is to enable adaptive and resilient systems for space, energy, and defense.

Design Across Scales

The behavior of all things in our macro world depend on a web of structural relationships which extend down to the micro-, nano-, molecular, and quantum scales. To solve engineering's toughest challenges, an understanding the dynamics across every scale is crucial.

Computational Methods

Utilizing multiphysics, quantum modeling, and machine learning algorithms, choices in materials and device design can be extensively explored and justified for application fitness before any material or instrument expenditure is made.

Multifunctional Behavior

Cross-Coupled Properties allow for changes in one domain (thermal/electrical/mechanical) to affect neighboring domains. By harnessing this phenomenon, materials can be tailored toward exceptional performance in sensing, energy harvesting, and computing.

Fabrication & Testing

The fabrication of electronic devices is as much a materials engineering problem as its design. By utilizing conventional and emerging fabrication techniques, informed by a strong materials understanding, electronic products can be produced reliably and at reasonable cost.
